We have made som Key Scenarios - with a little help for you to use.

KS1.

A blockbuster film premieres globally at 8:00 PM GMT. The platform must deliver a seamless experience to 5 million concurrent viewers across devices, with live interactions, real-time analytics, and zero downtime.

KS2.

A new user signs up during a high-traffic period. The system loads the onboarding flow in under 1 second, guides the user through profile creation with tooltips, and securely stores their preferences. The user receives personalized recommendations within 5 seconds of completing onboarding, even with 15,000 other users online.

KS3.

A user shares a film with 20 friends during a live premiere. The system processes the shares instantly, notifies friends in under 2 seconds, and filters out a spam comment automatically. The user’s watchlist remains private to their selected friends, and the system handles 1M+ interactions globally without slowdowns.

You can use the proposed scenarios attached or your own for the next step: Whiteboarding of an Application Overview.
